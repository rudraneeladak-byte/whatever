01 The Practice

A home in Kolkata is a memory you can live inside. My work is making sure it is also a sound decision.

Fewer homes. Fuller attention.

Soumee Adak Properties is a one-broker practice, kept deliberately small. No teams handing you off, no portals, no fifty open tabs. Each season I take on a handful of homes across south Kolkata and New Town, walk through every one of them myself, and show you only the few that deserve your evening.

When a home is right, I say so plainly. When it is priced wrong, I say that too, and then I negotiate as if the cheque were my own.

04 The Broker

Homes change hands. Trust should not.

I grew up off Southern Avenue, in a flat my grandfather bought on a handshake and kept for fifty years. That is the standard I hold every sale to. Twelve years in, the practice runs almost entirely on referral: families I moved once, calling again a decade later with a spare set of keys and a new question.

I represent a small number of sellers each season, and I bring them buyers who are ready. Everything goes in writing. Every number can face your lawyer, your banker, and your father-in-law. And I stay reachable long after possession day.
